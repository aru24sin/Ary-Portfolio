Case study 01 · 2025 – 26 · Client project

Frogwatch

Users rejected my first design. The second one shipped.

AI frog-call identification for citizen scientists. I led the team, designed it and built the front end.

Role
  • Team lead, UX designer, developer
Team
  • 4 students · Friends of the Rouge
Timeline
  • 2025 – 26 · Redesign 2026
Tools
  • Figma, React Native, Firebase
ProblemWhy it matters

Volunteers can hear frogs. Most can't name them.

Friends of the Rouge needed field data it could trust from people with no training.

Final designRecord to review

Record. Identify. Submit.

Decision 01Structure

One job per screen.

Testers rejected my all-in-one first design.

Instead ofEverything on one screen

Decision 02Visual language

Readable at dusk.

High contrast, 18pt text, thumb-sized targets.

Instead ofA light theme with small controls

Decision 03Trust

Show the frog. Let an expert decide.

A species photo beside the model's confidence. An expert confirms every submission.

Instead ofA thumbnail and a percentage

Decision 04Navigation

Record from anywhere.

The core action sits in the centre of the tab bar.

Instead ofOne of six equal home tiles

ImpactResults
94%task completion
2.1 tapsto start recording
8.2/10satisfaction

Measured on the shipped release. Screens show my 2026 redesign, not yet tested.

Key takeaway

I'd rather hear “no” in Figma than in production.
